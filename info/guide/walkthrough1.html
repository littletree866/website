Introduction

After the title screen, press the A Button or Start. A brief introduction to the world of Pokémon will be presented. Professor Oak, the region's leading researcher, appears and welcomes you to the world of Pokémon.

First, select your character's gender and enter a name (up to seven characters). Use the Control Pad to move the cursor and press A to confirm a letter; press B to erase the previous letter. After that, you will name your rival - the character who will challenge you throughout your journey. Once both names are set, you will receive your first Pokémon from Professor Oak's laboratory.

Pallet Town

Your journey begins in Pallet Town, a quiet village in the southern Kanto region. It has only a few buildings: your home to the west, your rival's house to the east, and Professor Oak's Laboratory to the south. The path north leads to Route 1, and the water to the south connects to Cinnabar Island.

When you head downstairs, your mom is sitting at the table. She tells you Professor Oak has been looking for you. To leave the house, step on the floor mat by the south wall.

Oak Appears!

Pallet Town is small. Professor Oak is not in the lab yet, so head north toward the tall grass. Suddenly, Oak stops you - it is dangerous to go alone! He takes you to his lab and offers you a choice of your first Pokémon. Each has strengths and weaknesses. Pick the one that fits your style.

  • Charmander (Fire): Strong against Grass, Bug, Ice, and Steel; weak to Water, Rock, and Ground. Its final form, Charizard, gains the Flying type, granting it Ground immunity but a Rock weakness.
  • Squirtle (Water): Excellent against Fire, Ground, and Rock. Weak to Electric and Grass. It is considered the best starter of the three. Its final form, Blastoise, has even better defenses and a wider movepool.
  • Bulbasaur (Grass/Poison): Although having the most weaknesses, it has a strong movepool and good stats. Its final form, Venusaur, is a powerful special attacker with excellent defenses.

After choosing, you may give your Pokémon a nickname. Your rival will choose the Pokémon that has a type advantage over yours. He then immediately challenges you to a battle.

Rival Battle 1

Professor Oak explains the basics: each Trainer sends out one Pokémon at a time, and they attack in turns until one faints. You can switch out, use items, or attack. Choose Fight to use a move; each Pokémon has two moves at this point. If your Pokémon's HP gets low, use a Potion from your Bag. Keep attacking until your rival's Pokémon faints.

Route 1

Route 1 is a peaceful road connecting Pallet Town to Viridian City. It is a straight path with no twists or curves, but it is a good place to encounter your first wild Pokémon.

Near the south end of Route 1, a man mentions the Poké Mart in Viridian City. When you visit, the clerk gives you a free Potion.

Viridian City

Viridian City is a small, verdant town. It features a Pokémon Center, a Poké Mart, and a Pokémon Gym (which is currently closed).

Pokémon Center

The Pokémon Center heals your team to full health and removes any status conditions. The nurse at the counter will heal your Pokémon for free. Speak to her for the first time to receive an Exp. Share. There is also a PC to access your storage.

Poké Mart

South of the Pokémon Center, this Mart sells essential items. Stock up before heading out.

Delivery

When you enter the Poké Mart, the clerk recognizes you from Pallet Town. Professor Oak's delivery arrived, and it's up to you to give the parcel to him.

Pokémon Academy

The Academy in the city center teaches new Trainers about battle basics. A blackboard explains the five status conditions:

StatusEffect
BurnAttack drops to 50%; loses 1/8 max HP each turn.
ParalysisSpeed drops to 25%; 25% chance to not attack.
PoisonLoses 1/8 max HP each turn; persists outside battle.
SleepCannot attack; lasts 1-7 turns.
FreezeCannot attack until thawed (20% each turn).

Oak's Lab (after delivering the Parcel)

After delivering Oak's Parcel, he gives you a Pokédex - a device that records data on every Pokémon you encounter. He also gives you five Poké Balls so you can start catching Pokémon. He wishes you luck and now you can officially start your journey.

Rival's House

Before leaving Pallet Town, visit your rival's house. His sister will give you a Town Map, useful for finding your way.

Back to Viridian City

With the Parcel delivered, the old man blocking the north path is now feeling better. He gives you a Quick Ball, and he will let you pass to Route 2. You can also head west to Route 22 for an optional rival battle.

Route 22

Route 22 is a short route west of Viridian City. It is not fully accessible yet, but you can encounter wild Pokémon and face your rival again.
