If you have either a Flying type or a Fire type Pokémon, you will have an easier time in this area. If your starter is Bulbasaur, you will have a significant disadvantage against the Bug-type trainers. Heal with the potions found in the forest after each battle if necessary. After defeating Bug Catcher Sammy, exit the forest to the north and continue to Pewter City.

Route 2 (North)

Route 2 (North) is the short stretch between Viridian Forest and Pewter City. Use the grass here if you need additional experience before entering the Gym.

Pewter City

Pewter City is home to the Pewter Gym and several useful facilities. Heal your team and prepare before challenging Brock.

Pokémon Center

The Pewter City Pokémon Center provides free healing and access to the PC. Speak to the man near the counter to receive a Quick Claw.

Pewter Gym

Pewter Gym specializes in Rock-type Pokémon. Brock's team is built around the high physical Defense commonly associated with Rock-types.

Strategy: Rock-type Pokémon are weak to Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground, and Steel-type attacks. If your starter is Squirtle or Bulbasaur, their STAB attacks provide an especially direct answer to Brock's team.

Gym Leader Brock

Brock uses Geodude and Onix. Both are Rock/Ground types, so Water, Grass, Fighting, Ground, Steel, and other appropriate coverage can be used to exploit their weaknesses.

Reward: Defeating Brock awards the Boulder Badge, ₽1,200 prize money, and TM39 Rock Tomb.

After the Battle

After defeating Brock, you receive the Boulder Badge and TM39 Rock Tomb. Check the badge description in-game for the current out-of-battle field-move requirements.

Before leaving Pewter City, consider:

  • Healing your Pokémon at the Pokémon Center.
  • Restocking Potions and status-healing items.
  • Checking the city's available NPC rewards and services.
  • Preparing your team for the trainers on Route 3.
